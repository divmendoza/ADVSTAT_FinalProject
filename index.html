Hypothesis Testing Z-Test

Enter your hypotheses, select the significance level, compute Z-statistics, visualize the normal curve with shaded acceptance and rejection regions, and get an automatic decision.

How this works

    - Choose H₀ and H₁ using dropdowns and set μ₀.
    - Enter sample mean x̄, population σ, and sample size n.
    - Select significance α. We support 0.10, 0.05, 0.01.
    - The app computes Z, shows critical value(s), shades the curve, and decides to reject or fail to reject H₀.

Assumptions

Z-test assumes known population standard deviation and normally distributed population (or large n via CLT).
